# 🔵 Digital Forensics Roadmap

A comprehensive learning path for becoming a proficient Digital Forensics Investigator, from fundamentals to advanced artifact analysis and DFIR.

**Estimated Duration:** 4–8 Months
**Target Role:** Digital Forensics Analyst / DFIR Specialist
**Prerequisites:** Networking, Windows, Linux, Security+
