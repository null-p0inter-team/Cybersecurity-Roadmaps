## Am I Ready?

To confirm whether you are ready or not, answer the following questions:

- Can you explain the OSI model and TCP/UDP protocols?
- Can you navigate Linux and Windows command lines confidently?
- Do you understand the difference between encoding, hashing, and encryption?
- Do you know the core CIA triad and common attack types?

If yes, you're ready to move to your specialized track.
